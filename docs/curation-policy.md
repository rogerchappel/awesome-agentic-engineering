# Curation policy

Awesome Agentic Engineering is a practical field guide, not a comprehensive directory.

## Include when

- The resource helps with agentic software engineering: code generation, repo context, orchestration, verification, evaluation, observability, secure execution, review, or release workflow.
- A practitioner can understand what to do next after clicking the link.
- The project or documentation has enough substance to evaluate.
- The description can be specific without borrowing marketing copy.

## Exclude when

- The entry is a thin wrapper with no clear engineering use case.
- The link is primarily lead capture, spam, or affiliate marketing.
- The project is abandoned and not historically important.
- The entry requires copying language or structure from another awesome list.
- The contribution is mostly ranking, cheerleading, or hype.

## Review questions

- What engineering decision does this help someone make?
- What safety, verification, or workflow gap does it address?
- Is the category still balanced after adding it?
- Does the description remain original and neutral?
